# Working on Storygro

- **Football Legends identity (owner, 2026-10-30):** every new or remade episode opens on frame zero with «Маленькие большие дела», «Легенды футбола» and the shared golden-football emblem, readable with faces inside the central preview square. Use the reusable `series-cover` scene and shared `production/styles/cartoon/football-legends.css`; narration starts immediately and the title yields to the story on a cue. Preserve Matvey/Sofia references. Give each episode its own researched venue, period kits, distinctive player/teammate faces, light, palette and story-specific actions; do not reuse one generic stadium or sepia look for the whole series. Use built-in GPT Image, as explicitly selected by the owner; record that the tool does not expose its exact model identifier. Episode 6 pilots a curiosity hook, visual changes on narrative beats, an answered opening question and an empathetic final question. This is an editorial experiment, not evidence of improved retention. See [series bible](content/series/football-legends.md) and [recipe](docs/how-to/football-series.md). This standing design direction does not grant owner script/storyboard/release approval.

- **Reels learning loop (owner-adopted workflow, 2026-10-31):** every new or deliberately remade Reel must use [the retention recipe](docs/how-to/reels-retention.md) and complete `DIRECTION.md` before asset production: viewer promise, three opening alternatives, selected opening, early result, causal beats, payoff and one experiment. Show a consequence early, build connected actions, resolve the opening promise and end with one useful takeaway and at most one natural question. Rich detail, a sourced nickname and a playful visual idea support a clear foreground; preserve historical likeness, kits and venue. Review the silent opening at phone size, then inspect cues and listen on the actual timeline. Preserve supplied scripts when verbatim was requested. The next Football Legends episode uses **FL-R1: action beneath the opening identity**, as recorded in [the experiment plan](content/research/reels-growth-2026-10-31/experiments.yaml); hold the duration band and narrator fixed. Change one main factor per pilot; 5–8-second early rewards and a later 45–60-second length test remain editorial hypotheses, not platform requirements or proven uplift. Measure the actual published version around 24h, 72h and 7d, retaining actual age and distribution; views are not reach, shares are not automatically sends, and average watch time divided by length is not completion. Keep missing metrics absent and inconclusive results visible. See [Sivori research and external examples](content/research/reels-growth-2026-10-31/README.md). Adoption of this workflow is not an episode approval or publication authorisation.

- **Children's pacing and pronunciation (owner-adopted, 2026-10-31, after Eusebio V2):** keep speech calm and allow time to inspect the picture; hooks and retention goals must not cause accelerated narration or cuts before a child can understand a result. Hold an important score/reveal after its spoken phrase; use roughly 1–2 seconds as a review starting point, not an automatic interval for every shot. Eusebio V2 is the working reference (same OmniVoice B, 0.82/off, final 5:3 held 1.60 seconds after speech); preserve each episode's explicit settings and the shared narrator reference. Check photo dwell time and every meaningful transition on the actual narrated timeline at phone size. Verify difficult words and homographs in context, record sourced norms in the shared lexicon and repair only the affected pronunciation; ASR/phoneme checks are supporting evidence, not a listening approval. Record pace changes as experiment confounders. See [the retention recipe](docs/how-to/reels-retention.md#pace-for-children) and [series recipe](docs/how-to/football-series.md). This adopts production rules, not an owner release approval.
- **Instagram Reels standard (owner, 2026-10-29):** every new or remade Instagram Reel uses native **1080×1920, 9:16, 30 fps**, with a full-frame background and **no added side bars, letterboxing or shrinking the complete edit into a safe rectangle**. Start with `storygro new <folder> --style <id> --lang <lang> --target instagram-reels`. Use `safe: reels` for important text; captions normally have at most four words per phrase, respect the safe width and sit above the lower UI. Instagram also shows the file cropped: 4:5 in the feed (285 px off the top and bottom), 3:4 on the grid and 1:1 in link previews. So text stays ≥320 px from the top (`reels` enforces it), heads and faces sit below the feed line in every shot, and the cover and first frame keep their title and faces inside the central square; check with `still --guides` (amended 2026-10-29 after the owner saw the published Matthews Reel cut at the top). Compose faces and action around titles/cards; move or resize individual overlays, or recompose the illustration. Inspect every shot/cue and caption, the actual MP4 including first/last frames, QA's `feed-preview.jpg` and `cover-preview.jpg`, and listen before release. **Upload (owner-confirmed 2026-10-29, after four uploads Instagram stored as squares):** upload as a Reel (app: + → Reel; Post flow or website: crop icon → Original), check that the preview is tall, set the delivered cover, then run `storygro instagram-check <link>`; it must report 1080×1920. Judge a published post by that measurement, not by its square 640×640 Open Graph thumbnail. `deliver` writes the steps to `UPLOAD.txt`. The organic safe margins are project estimates. `social` padding is only for an explicitly requested bordered derivative, never the default Reels repair. Apply this standard when revising existing Instagram episodes; do not silently rewrite their selected voice or approvals. See [ADR 0007](docs/adr/0007-instagram-reels.md) and [the production recipe](docs/how-to/instagram-without-cropping.md).

- **Public Git media rule (owner, 2026-10-19):** never commit videos, audio, animated GIFs, archives, rendered PDF/PPTX, model weights or generated caches. Keep them in ignored local storage or release/object storage. Every indexed file must be ≤1 MiB; documentation/review preview images ≤300 KiB. Do not bypass this with `git add -f`, Git LFS, renamed extensions or an ad hoc allowlist. Source scripts, facts, provenance, licences and small previews belong in Git. See [media policy](docs/reference/media-policy.md).
- Install the local guard with `npm run hooks:install`; `npm run check` and CI enforce the same policy against **indexed blobs**, including forced additions. Use `npm run assets:fetch` to restore original off-Git render inputs from the checksum manifest. Never silently replace a differing local original.
- Social publication is a future explicit operation with a named destination and release. Local `deliver` and `package` are not publication.
- Every on-screen number lives in a data file with a quoted source (`sources.md`); never type numbers into scenes. Label predictions as predictions and keep the source's caveats.
- Narration is synthetic: disclose it in post texts. Never clone a real person's voice without their written consent. Model-provided voices may come from consented human performers; preserve accurate provenance rather than claiming no real person supplied the voice. Check model terms ([voices](docs/reference/voices.md)).
