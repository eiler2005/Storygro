# Changelog

Notable changes to the Storygro engine and repository. Content releases (videos) are tagged separately on [GitHub Releases](https://github.com/eiler2005/Storygro/releases) as `<episode>-<date>`. Dates are when the change reached `main`.

## [Unreleased]

### Video formats and the shared recipe
- [Video formats](docs/explanation/video-formats.md): seven formats made so far — business explainer, news collage, product demo, kinetic short, cartoon explainer, high-quality cartoon with GPT Image, children's list cartoon — with what each is for, its style and scenes, where its pictures come from, a picture and an example; how to choose; what GPT Image costs and what to watch.
- [Workflow](docs/explanation/workflow.md#from-an-idea-to-a-video-on-the-phone): the twelve steps from a brief to the owner's phone and Git, who does each (owner, main agent, helper model, voice and image models) and when to move on; a length budget measured on the Russian episodes and stage times on one machine.
- `npm run docs:media` builds `docs/media/formats/gpt-image.jpg` from the footballers cartoon's pictures; [how we work](docs/explanation/how-we-work.md) adds the picture model to the model table; the docs index, README and feature pages link the formats and the new episodes.

### Registry, name-aware stress lookup and the footballers cartoon
- `docs/reference/registry.md`, generated by `npm run docs:registry` (`production/scripts/registry-doc.ts`; a test fails while it is stale): every reel with its scene sequence and approval, every style's story structure, every scene with the reels that use it, runtime helpers, theme tokens and the visual QA checks.
- `storygro stress --lookup` searches a name together with the capitalised words around it first («Лука Модрич» → Лу́ка, not the archbishop's Лука́). Dictionary entries for every name of the new episode; `mark: false` and phoneme checks for «Золотой», «Золотых» and «Лионель» after the cartoon kept wrong takes.
- Picture provider `codex`: GPT Image through the Codex CLI's image tool (`images.provider: codex`, `images.model`), batched and parallel, cropped to the reel's size, recorded as not seed-reproducible. Chosen over Z-Image for recognisable, drawing-like pictures after a side-by-side test.
- Scenes: `illustration` takes `badge.y` (keep a face clear) and shrinks a one-word headline that cannot wrap to fit its band; `kinetic` marks a slamming word `data-rc-safe-ignore` while it is still larger than its final size.
- QA speech check: names that Russian ASR spells as it hears them count (joined words, small spelling differences on long words); number words keep their own rules.
- Qwen3 no longer gets dictionary marks except entries marked `mark: always` (the owner found unmarked Qwen3 cleaner; plain words suffered when every name was marked); its lines key as before, so earlier cached voice returns. The children's list cartoon is written down as a method ([how-to](docs/how-to/kids-list-cartoon.md)) with the series `kids-football`.
- Children's cartoon «Звёзды футбола: собираем альбом» (`content/videos/football-stars-kids`): 14 players, an album overlay with a progress rail, `kinetic` chapter cards inside a cartoon reel; reference notes on the reel it took its format from.

### Russian stress dictionary and visual checks
- A shared pronunciation dictionary, `production/voices/lexicon-ru.yaml`: names and hard words with their stress, case forms and a source each, applied to every Russian line before RUAccent's homographs (the author's marks still win). The voice cache keys a line on the entries it uses, so a new entry re-voices only its lines; the resolved dictionary reaches `py/voice.py` through the job, so Python needs no YAML.
- `storygro stress <spec> [--lookup] [--write]`: dictionary words, author marks and capitalised words nobody checked, per scene; `--lookup` finds the stress of exactly that word form in ru.wikipedia and ru.wiktionary (declension tables), `--write` appends entries with the quote. The plan flags unchecked words per scene.
- Phoneme checks (`py/phonemes.py`, wav2vec2-xlsr-53-espeak-cv-ft): a dictionary word with a `check` that comes out wrong is spoken again with another seed, up to three takes; QA check «Dictionary words keep their stress» listens again in the final voice track. The voice bench uses the same module.
- QA and the storyboard check «Text fits its box»: no text may stick out of the card, band or label it sits in. It caught years wider than their badge in vertical cartoons; the `illustration` badge now shrinks its number to fit.

### Motion, kinetic type and four new scenes
- Motion kit in `production/runtime/kit.js`: closed-form `spring` and `springs` (one spring per change of target, summed), the music's beat grid as `beatGrid`, and `ctx.beats` in every scene (`at`, `next`, `snap`, `index`, `pulse`) — the same grid `score.py` plays. Sound effects take `snap: beat | half | bar`.
- Motion blur: `format.blur: N` captures N frames per output frame across a 180° shutter and averages them with ffmpeg `tmix`; `render --blur N` overrides it, `--draft` ignores it, the manifest records it.
- Style `kinetic-type` (pilot): vertical shorts made of words on a dark field, captions off, blur 4; theme tokens `--kt-*`.
- Scenes `kinetic` (each word lands when it is spoken, an accent bar travels between lines and pulses on the beat, `swap` turns a word from thin to heavy), `timeline`, `process` (isometric stations with a travelling token) and `scale` (areas proportional to values, the camera pulls back). Shared tokens and word matching in `production/runtime/type.js`.
- `images.prompt` in a reel replaces the style's picture prompt for one episode (must keep `{subject}`).
- Reference notes on [awesome-opus5-5-videos](content/research/references/awesome-opus5-5-videos.md): what the catalogue of 703 code-rendered video prompts confirms about the renderer and which motion principles we take; motion principles in the [scene contract](docs/reference/scene-contract.md#motion).
- New episodes: the kinetic-type pilot [«Как агент собирает ролик из кода»](content/videos/agent-motion-studio/README.md) and the children's cartoon [«Золотой мяч: кто самый лучший?»](content/videos/ballon-dor-kids/README.md).

### Voices: Russian stress, more engines, a bench
- Russian stress layer (`production/py/stress.py`): hand marks `зам+ок` / `замо́к`, and by default RUAccent resolves the homographs in `production/py/homographs-ru.txt` from the sentence (plus ё in все/всё-type pairs); each engine gets the marks in the form it reads, or none. `voice.stress: auto | marks | off`. The voice log, the line JSON and the plan list every decision. `+` is no longer read aloud by Qwen3 as «плюс».
- New engines: `chatterbox` (Chatterbox Multilingual, MIT, clones the narrator; `npm run setup:chatterbox`) and `yandex` (SpeechKit API v1; request unit-tested, not yet run against the live service).
- `production/py/voice_bench.py` with `production/voices/bench-ru.yaml`: the same lines through every candidate — Whisper WER, homograph stress from phonemes over several takes, brand names, UTMOS, words a second, compute time — and a page to listen. Results and the decision are in [voices](docs/reference/voices.md#russian-voices-compared).
- `npm run storage` and [local setup](docs/reference/local-setup.md): what Storygro keeps on the machine, sizes, what must not be deleted and how to restore the rest.

### Content workflow
- Each episode keeps its review in Git: `review.yaml` with open questions, notes and three approvals — script, storyboard, release — each with a hash of what it covers; any later change shows as "changed since approval". `storygro approve --step …`; agents record technical approvals with `--agent`. The storyboard approval moved from the ignored `.reel/approved.json` (still read) to `review.yaml`.
- `storygro plan <spec>`: `PLAN.<variant>.md` with a strip per scene (rendered frame + pictures), narration, on-screen text, facts with sources, approvals and questions; small previews in `preview/plan/`.
- `storygro tracker <videos-dir>`: every episode's stage, whose move, approvals, length, QA, sourced facts, questions and publications, with the owner's waiting list (`content/TRACKER.md`).
- A reel can set `images.width/height` (vertical pictures for 9:16 cartoons).
- First episode made this way: the chase cartoon [«Не бунт, а открытая дверь»](content/videos/agents-open-door/README.md) and the [«Открытая дверь» series bible](content/series/open-door.md) with fourteen sourced plots.

### Research scan
- `storygro trends` gains topical sources without API keys: Hacker News best of the week, new GitHub repositories by stars, Hugging Face daily papers, public Telegram channels (views against the channel median) and RSS/Atom feeds; X digests from Grok import with `--inbox` (bookmarks, likes, save rate).
- Sifting: `block:` drops unwanted items, `hype:` sets loud headlines aside, the same repository/paper/page across sources is marked as an echo, and a short **Picks** list orders reading (one per story, a cap per author).
- [Grok prompt for the X digest](production/trends/x-digest-prompt.md) and an [angles playbook](production/trends/angles-prompt.md) that turns picks into backlog candidates.

### Toolkit
- A bank of every method and tool for making content: [`production/toolkit.yaml`](production/toolkit.yaml) (44 entries — methods, tools, styles, scene families, providers — across nine stages) and the generated [toolkit page](docs/reference/toolkit.md) (`npm run docs:toolkit`). Tests fail when a CLI command, style or scene is missing from it, a path does not exist or the page is stale.

### Styles, pictures and transcripts
- **Styles** as a unit of reuse: `production/styles/<id>/` with defaults, theme, scenes, pacing, script structure and picture settings; reels opt in with `style:`. Three styles: `cartoon` (new), `cut-paper`, `editorial-collage` ([ADR 0005](docs/adr/0005-styles-and-generated-pictures.md)).
- `storygro styles`, `storygro new <folder> --style <id>` (a project from the style's template).
- The `illustration` scene: generated pictures that change on cues with a slow camera move, a split frame, and DOM overlays — headline band, number badge, labels, source line.
- `storygro images`: local picture generation with mflux and Z-Image-Turbo (8-bit) in a separate `~/.storygro/image-venv` (`npm run setup:images`), a recurring cast via `@name`, stable seeds, and `images.json` provenance (prompt, seed, model, licence, SHA-256). Pictures stay out of Git.
- `storygro transcript`: pasted page transcripts, media files and direct media URLs (page URLs with a user-installed yt-dlp) → JSON, Markdown, SRT and pace statistics.
- Reference analysis of an 18-minute illustrated finance explainer, and the first cartoon short, [The AI industry is buying what you know about your job](content/videos/ai-buys-work-knowledge/README.md) (EN, ~1 min).

### Repository and documentation
- Documentation restructured by Diátaxis: `docs/tutorials/`, `how-to/`, `reference/`, `explanation/`, an index that routes by goal, and new [start here](docs/start-here.md), [features](docs/features.md) and [examples](docs/examples.md) pages ([ADR 0004](docs/adr/0004-docs-structure-and-media.md)).
- The long workflows page split into a workflow overview and nine how-to guides; new [CLI reference](docs/reference/cli.md), [repository layout](docs/reference/repository-layout.md) and [how we work](docs/explanation/how-we-work.md) (roles, model tiers, where files go, how a change reaches Git).
- English pictures throughout: a scene gallery of all eighteen scenes, a hero, an English storyboard strip, a pipeline diagram (SVG) and English pipeline panels, all rebuilt by `npm run docs:media` from local renders. New `examples/scene-gallery/` provides English frames for `chart`, `collage` and `screencast`.
- Community files: CONTRIBUTING, CODE_OF_CONDUCT, SECURITY, ROADMAP, this changelog, issue and PR templates, CODEOWNERS, Dependabot, `.editorconfig`.

### Checks
- `npm run check:links` (part of `npm run check`) fails on relative links or heading anchors that do not resolve.
- The media guard now applies the 300 KiB preview limit to every image under `docs/`, not only `docs/media/`.

## [0.2.0] — 2026-10-19

### Production
- Native 9:16 and other presets, platform safe areas with `--guides`, language variants as sibling `<lang>.reel.yaml` files.
- Voice providers behind one interface: Kokoro (EN), Kokoro-RU with RUAccent, Qwen3-TTS on Apple silicon, Silero; Whisper alignment for engines without word times.
- Cut-paper explainer scenes (`headline`, `crowd`, `task-stack`, `ladder`, `stat`, `chart`, `steps`, `outro`), the editorial `collage` scene, and `screencast` for real screen recordings.
- `storygro capture`: scripted phone-browser recording in virtual time with the finger path.
- Carousels, post texts, `episode.yaml` and `storygro package` with manifest, SHA-256, engine commit, voice provenance and QA verdicts.
- Stricter QA: facts gate, safe areas at every cue, Whisper speech check (numbers ↔ digits, «процент» ↔ %), brand rules.

### Editorial
- `storygro ideas` (editorial ranking) and `storygro trends` (Google Trends RSS, Wikipedia, Hacker News without API keys; a watchlist with momentum per backlog idea).
- Brand profile (`content/brand.yaml`) and series (`content/series/`, `storygro series`).
- Manual publication ledger and metric snapshots (`storygro publish`, `storygro metrics`); nothing is uploaded automatically.

### Repository
- Content separated from production: `content/` for videos, ideas and research; `production/` for the engine ([ADR 0003](docs/adr/0003-content-production-boundary.md)).
- Public Git media policy enforced on indexed blobs by a pre-commit hook and CI; large render inputs restored by `npm run assets:fetch` from a checksum manifest.
- PolyForm Shield 1.0.0 licence, following SlideCraft.
- Renderer decision recorded after a HyperFrames pilot ([ADR 0001](docs/adr/0001-renderer.md)); voice providers in [ADR 0002](docs/adr/0002-voice-providers.md).

### Fixed
- A Chrome launch race (DevTools port read before it was written).

## [0.1.0] — 2026-10-18

- Storygro extracted from SlideCraft's launch-video tooling as its own repository: the DOM/CDP renderer with deterministic `draw(t)` scenes, voice-first timing with cues, storyboard approval, music and effects synthesis, and the historical SlideCraft launch film as the first example.
- Research, product decisions, implementation plan and handoff documented in `docs/planning/`.
