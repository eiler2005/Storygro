# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Storygro to be a welcoming project for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience or opinion. We pledge to make participation harassment-free.

## Our standards

Good: being respectful and constructive, giving and accepting feedback gracefully, assuming good intent, focusing on what is best for creators and viewers.

Not acceptable: harassment, insults or personal attacks, discriminatory language or imagery, publishing others' private information, and any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions — and when representing the project elsewhere.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer by email at [denis.ermilov@gmail.com](mailto:denis.ermilov@gmail.com), via a [GitHub security advisory](https://github.com/eiler2005/Storygro/security/advisories/new), or as a direct message to [@eiler2005](https://github.com/eiler2005). Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove contributions and restrict participation for violations.
