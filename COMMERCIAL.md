## Uses covered without a separate paid agreement

- Creating videos, carousels, social posts and other content for yourself, your employer or paying clients.
- Using Storygro internally at a company, including through agents, scripts and CI.
- Adapting the software for permitted purposes, including internal workflows and brand themes.
- Sharing permitted copies or modifications with the licence and its Required Notice lines.

Commercial content creation is allowed for permitted, noncompeting uses. Storygro's software licence does not claim ownership of your generated content. Rights and restrictions from your inputs, third-party assets, fonts, models and voice providers still apply.

## Uses that need a separate agreement when they compete

Shield excludes providing a product that competes with the software or with a product the licensor provides using it. For Storygro, examples can include a competing hosted content-generation service, a competing white-label video/agent product, or distribution marketed as a practical substitute for Storygro. Free competing services can also fall within that restriction.

Paid client video work does not by itself become a competing software service. Embedding Storygro in another product needs assessment of what the resulting product provides; not every paid integration is automatically competition. Write to the contact above for a licence covering a competing use case or for a written answer on your proposed integration.
