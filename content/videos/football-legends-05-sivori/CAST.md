## Juventus venue

The historical setting is **Stadio Comunale, Turin**, Juventus's home during Sivori's tenure. References show the open oval, concrete exterior piers, athletics track, rectangular Marathon tower with its narrow vertical window strip, period floodlights and Turin streets. The establishing illustration follows the period aerial postcard; action backgrounds use these recognisable elements as an artistic interpretation, not a surveyed reconstruction of every interior tier or camera angle. Host scenes are fictional training illustrations around the stadium, not a claim to document the exact location of a practice field.

No modern Allianz Stadium roof/cables, Delle Alpi pylons, LED adverts or contemporary kits are used as a 1961 setting. Cool blue/silver daylight, charcoal/off-white overlays, burgundy opponents and a new musical progression distinguish this episode from the earlier golden-hour scenes. Names, years and labels are separate data-bound overlays, never generated lettering.
