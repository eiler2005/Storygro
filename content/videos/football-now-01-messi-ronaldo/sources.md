## Editorial limits

Respect for opponents, officials and teammates is the programme's lesson, not a claim that either player has always behaved perfectly. No training regimen, diet, body measurements, invented player quotations or forecast of future records. Current-style kits use verified club colours without pretending to reproduce every2026/27 sponsor/detail. The2012 scene uses the period Barcelona palette and pre-redevelopment Camp Nou; all staged actions are fictional demonstrations. Current facts must be rechecked before a later publication.
