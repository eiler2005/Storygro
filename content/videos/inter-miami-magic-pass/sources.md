## Caveats

- Messi’s eight Ballon d’Or awards are stated as of the research date, before the scheduled 2026 ceremony.
- Suárez shared the 2014 European Golden Shoe with Cristiano Ronaldo; his other award was in 2016. The “golden boots” joke is explicitly about trophies.
- Casemiro’s five Champions League titles were with Real Madrid, not Inter Miami.
- St. Clair won the 2025 goalkeeper award at Minnesota United. His move to Miami is confirmed by [Inter Miami](https://www.intermiamicf.com/news/inter-miami-cf-signs-2025-mls-goalkeeper-of-the-year-dayne-st-clair). The film does not call him the current season’s award winner.
- Berterame was born in Argentina, became a Mexican citizen and represents Mexico; the on-screen arrow is biographical, not a transfer between national teams.
- Masha’s trial, glowing passing lanes and training lessons are our fictional framing. No invented quotation is attributed to a footballer.
- Photographs come from the official player profile pages listed in images.json. Copyright remains with the relevant third-party rights holders; no open licence is asserted. They are kept in ignored local storage.
