## Editorial boundaries

- Both clubs are Moscow clubs; derby is explained through local rivalry, not as an exhaustive definition of all derbies.
- Spartak foundation is 1922; CSKA traces its origins to OLLS in 1911. Neither modern name nor modern kit is claimed to date unchanged from foundation.
- Red-white and red-blue are traditional club colours; kits vary.
- The 2005 score is CSKA 3, Sporting 1, in the UEFA Cup final; not a derby score.
- Friends, dialogue, football action and tactical example are invented. No outcome or current player roster is asserted.
- Images are symbolic AI reconstructions. Historical kits, museum, trophy and stadium are illustrative, not exact archival records or official replicas. All frames carry a reconstruction or fictional-example label.
- Tactical recommendation is only for the shown example, not a permanent style assigned to either club. Family tradition is an illustrative possibility, not a survey result.
