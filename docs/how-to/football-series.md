## Cartoon-led short stories

The [Football Wow plan](../../content/series/football-wow.md) preserves the supplied short scripts and their numbering. Produce one commissioned episode at a time. Before assets, complete the retention direction worksheet and fact-check the single surprise; preserve the original text separately from corrections/adaptations.

Use approximately 25 seconds as a writing constraint, keeping the selected calm narrator. Build several original illustrated states of the same action: setup, technique, obstacle, shot, consequence and emotional payoff. Vary camera size, preserve screen direction and check period face/kit/ball/trophy/venue. Use the approved smaller hosts as supporting characters. Record one visual idea and the episode's own light/palette; no generic repeating stadium or photo-card sequence.

Limit the whole film to 1–3 authentic photo inserts, with drawings occupying the clear majority of runtime. Count both unique photographs and actual insert occurrences; record measured photo and illustrated screen time. Keep a photo's original pixels and date; full attribution belongs in the post/docs. A tool limit does not authorise a photographic substitute for the requested cartoon.

Use existing `series-cover`, `illustration` and `archive-photo` modules where suitable. The new rubric uses its exact compact owner-supplied identity at frame zero, followed by connected artwork/cues without a separate logo pre-roll. Clear space for captions and important faces in each composition; check all actual cuts and the exported MP4. Label a sequence of animated illustrations accurately; do not claim full skeletal character animation when only pose states/camera motion were produced.
