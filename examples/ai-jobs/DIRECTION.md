# Direction — AI and jobs

> **Audience:** whoever edits the scenes or the script of this episode

**The feeling:** calm, clear, slightly uneasy. Not "robots are coming"; a viewer recognises a quiet change they may already see at work and leaves with something to do.

**Arc:** surprise (no mass layoffs, yet fewer hires) → reframe (exposed is not replaced) → mechanism (a job is a stack of tasks; AI cuts the checkable ones) → the same cards become ladder rungs that disappear → evidence (US payroll data) → the danger (no seniors in ten years) → what to do → the line to remember.

**Visual language — cut paper on a desk.**
- Warm paper (`--pp-paper`) with grain and a few torn scraps at the edges; flat cut-out shapes with a crisp contact shadow; nothing glossy.
- AI is a pair of red scissors: it cuts tasks out, it does not destroy the job. Tasks are paper strips; career levels are wooden rungs — the strips the scissors cut are the rungs that later fall.
- Recurring objects: the junior figure (teal, backpack strap), the experienced figure (blue, glasses), the scissors, the ladder. The ladder returns after the data, already cut, so the viewer recognises it.
- Type: Unbounded for claims, Golos Text for labels and captions, Caveat for handwritten notes. Red is the change, teal the safe/kept, yellow the marker.
- Motion: cut-outs drop and settle (`drop`), words rise one by one, markers sweep, stamps slam. Slow camera drift while holding. Every change happens on a cue in the narration.
- Captions: a paper strip at the bottom of the safe area, five words a phrase, the spoken word inked.

## Scenes (cues)

1. **hook — headline.** Frame 0: "Не увольняют." / "No mass layoffs." `next`: the red lines rise, the marker sweeps under the last word, the stamp "набор закрыт" / "hiring paused" slams in.
2. **risk — crowd.** 100 figures in a 20 × 5 band. `share`: the high-risk group (RU: 1 in 10, HSE; EN: 1 in 4, ILO) lights up and the big number drops in; EN `top`: the 3 % highest-exposure figures turn red; the handwritten note says most jobs change rather than vanish (RU: full automation < 1 %).
3. **tasks — task-stack.** A junior analyst's day as six strips. `cut`: scissors snip the three checkable tasks (data, first draft, meeting summary), which fly away; the rest close up. `after`: "Same job. Different tasks."
4. **ladder — ladder.** The same kinds of tasks as rungs, the junior on the ground, others above. `cut`: the bottom two rungs and the rails under them fall; the junior on the first rung drops to the ground. `gap`: a red line and "no first rung".
5. **data — stat.** Two torn cards: −11 % (22–25, most exposed) and +10 % (22–25, least exposed), source line with the caveat. `c`: "not fired — not hired".
6. **danger — ladder (instant).** The cut ladder is back. `future`: stamp "in 10 years", the senior leaves the top, a question mark stays.
7. **todo — steps.** Four numbered cards drop on `a`–`d`, each gets a hand-drawn tick.
8. **outro.** The line to remember; `cta`: the handwritten call to send it to someone starting out; the newcomer climbs onto the first rung of a small ladder.

Sound: a soft procedural bed (energy 0.25–0.45, no drums), a thud on the hook and the gap, whooshes on the cuts, pops on drops, a swell under "in ten years", a chime on the call to action.
